Homelab Backups

A private backup utility operated by TDStrikers for their own home server.

The utility creates system and application backups, encrypts them locally, and uploads the encrypted files to the owner's Google Drive. Google authorization is used for unattended backup uploads, integrity checks, and recovery downloads.

The utility requests access only to Drive files and folders it creates. It is intended solely for the owner's personal use and does not offer public registration.
