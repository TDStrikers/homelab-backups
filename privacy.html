Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026. Homelab Backups is a personal utility operated by TDStrikers.

Google Drive access

The utility uses Google OAuth to create, upload, list, verify, and download backup files within the files and folders created by this connection. It requests the drive.file permission, rather than access to all existing Drive files. Backup metadata, including names, sizes and integrity information, is processed to manage backups and validate recovery.

Storage and protection

Backup contents and filenames are encrypted on the home server before upload. Temporary plaintext archives may exist locally until successful upload verification. Authorization credentials and encryption keys are stored privately on the owner's equipment. An independent recovery copy of the encryption credentials is required.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used solely to provide the owner's backup and recovery functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Encrypted backups are stored with Google Drive under the owner's account. The utility does not intentionally share them with other parties.

Retention and control

Backups remain in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them or configures a retention policy. Access can be revoked through the Google Account third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing backups. The owner controls deletion of local credentials and cloud backup files.

This website

These informational pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, or advertising. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request information according to its own policies.
